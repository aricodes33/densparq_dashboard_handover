# Venue KPI definitions

Conventions: `avail_ch` = open-hours x courts minus maintenance/closure blocks (fixed rule, documented). Revenue = recognised court revenue, net of refunds. Timestamps in venue-local tz. [D] = derived/recommended convention, not from a cited source.

| KPI | Formula | Include / exclude | Grain | Pitfall | Benchmark | Src |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Occupancy / utilisation | booked_ch / avail_ch | Num: confirmed+played bookings incl. member-included, comps, coaching, league/tournament [D]; no-shows count as booked (slot was unsold to others). Excl: cancelled. Den: exclude maintenance/closure/private-event blocks (hotel "out of order" analogue); keep owner holds in den but report separately [D]. Compute per 30-min slot, then roll up | slot -> day/court -> week/month | Mixing den conventions across venues; hotel sources split on OOO treatment, so store the rule; blended average hides peak/off-peak | Padel: 50%+ cited as profitability target; ~65% avg and 85%+ top (vendor blogs); UK ~85% peak (secondary). Pickleball indoor: peak 55/70/85%+, off-peak 15/30/50%+ (CourtReserve calculator, self-reported) | 1,2,3,4 |
| Revenue per available court-hour (RevPACH) | court_revenue / avail_ch = ARR x occupancy | Revenue: booking fees + allocated membership revenue [D]; excl: F&B, retail, tax, tournament entry (report "total facility rev per court-hr" separately) | day/week/month | Counting membership cash on purchase date, not allocated over validity; mixing tax-inclusive prices | Pickleball: median PodPlay club ~$8,000/month/court (vendor case study). No credible India/padel benchmark found | 4,5,6 |
| Avg realised hourly rate (ARR; hotel ADR analogue) | court_revenue / booked_ch | Net of discounts, comps (zero rev) in den or reported both ways; allocated membership revenue per [D] | week/month; by peak band | Rack rate vs realised rate confusion; excl comps flatters ARR | CourtReserve: $25/$40/$60 low/median/top indoor (self-reported); no India benchmark found | 4,6 |
| Cancellation rate | cancelled_bookings / (confirmed + cancelled) created in period; late-cancel = cancelled inside policy window (e.g. <24h) / cancelled | Excl admin/venue-initiated cancels (weather, maintenance) - report separately | week/month | Cohort by booking-created vs play-date inconsistently; late cancels with refunds hide revenue loss | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| No-show rate | no_shows / (completed + no_shows) | Needs check-in/attendance flag; excl venue cancellations | week/month | Without check-in data no-shows are unobservable; do not infer from payment | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Booking lead time | median(start_time - created_at) in hours/days | Excl recurring series instances after first, admin-created | month; by band | Mean skewed by recurring/season bookings: use median and percentiles | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| New vs returning | new = customer's first-ever completed booking falls in period; returning = had one before period start | Dedupe by customer_id (phone/email merge); guests excluded unless account exists | week/month | Duplicate identities inflate "new"; group-booking participants vs booker | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Repeat rate | customers with >=2 completed bookings in window / customers with >=1 in window (also 30/60/90d repeat from first booking) | Excl cancelled | rolling 90d/12m | Window mismatch; tenure-biased for older customers | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Retention / cohort | cohort = first-booking month; retention_k = cohort customers active in month k / cohort size | Active = >=1 completed booking | monthly cohorts, 12 months | Right-censoring of recent cohorts; small cohorts noisy | Fitness (membership, not court-booking): ~66.4% annual retention cited to HFA 2025 report via secondary blogs (unverified at source) | 7,8 |
| Customer churn | lapsed / active at start; lapsed = no completed booking in N days (N ~ 2x median inter-booking gap) [D] | Excl new in period | monthly | Arbitrary N; seasonal sports (monsoon, summer) | Fitness annual attrition ~28.6% older IHRSA figure (secondary) - not court-booking | 8 |
| Membership churn / renewal rate | churn = members expiring not renewed within grace / members due; renewal = 1 - churn (renewals / expiries due in period) | Excl cancelled-early shown separately; exclude trials/ comps | monthly, by plan | Counting auto-pause/freeze as churn; mixing fixed-term vs monthly plans | Gym: 62-68% full-service annual, 74-82% PT studios (vendor blogs, unverified) | 7,8 |
| Membership utilisation | member_included_ch used / entitlement hrs (or avg bookings per active member/month); also % members with 0 use in 30d | Active members only; per plan | monthly | Heavy-use members subsidised; zero-use is churn leading indicator | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Customer LTV (simple) | ARPU/month x gross margin % / monthly churn; or avg rev per booking x bookings per customer per year x expected years | Net revenue; payments table, refunds netted | quarterly | Using churn from short window; undiscounted; ignoring acquisition cost | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Discount redemption rate / depth | redemption = bookings using offer / bookings eligible (or offers issued); depth = discount_amount / (list_price) | List price from price rule at booking time; excl comps (report separately) | per campaign; weekly | Counting redemptions by customers who would book anyway (no uplift); need list-price snapshot | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Booking source mix | source_bookings / total_bookings by source (app, web, walk-in/admin, marketplace, phone) | Count and revenue-weighted | month | Missing/"unknown" source; marketplace commission not netted | No credible benchmark found | [D] |
| Peak vs off-peak | Define by data: slots where occupancy >= venue's 75th percentile (trailing 8 weeks), or price-rule bands; typical padel/pickleball peak = weekday evenings + weekends | Bands stored per venue/day-type | recalc quarterly | Using price bands that lag demand; holidays | CourtReserve typical indoor 55-75% peak vs 25-40% off-peak | 4 |
| Unfilled capacity | avail_ch - booked_ch (hours) and x ARR = revenue opportunity; by band | same as occupancy den | day/week | Treating all unfilled as recoverable (late-night slots) | n/a | [D] |
