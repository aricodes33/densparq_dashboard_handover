# Academy assessment and KPIs

## 1. Principles
- Use both: a 1-5 behaviourally anchored rubric (coach-scored, covers technique/tactics/attitude) plus 3-5 objective drill tests per sport. Tests give numbers; the rubric gives context. [1][5]
- Baseline at enrolment, then re-test every 8-12 weeks (6 weeks minimum for fast-changing under-10s). Fewer cycles are cheaper; more than 6-weekly mostly measures noise. (Practice recommendation; no federation-mandated interval found.)
- Compare only within age band + level (+ sex for 12+). Same-age children can differ 3-5 years in maturation; early maturers can lead by up to 4 years. Chronological age is a poor grouping guide. [2]
- Age bands (Sport for Life, general guide; sport-specific ages vary): Learn to Train M 9-12 / F 8-11 (learn all basic skills before puberty); Train to Train M 12-16 / F 11-15. Suggested platform bands: U8, U10, U12, U14, U16, U18, Adult. [1][2]
- Label every value `measured` (timed/counted with protocol) or `estimated` (coach judgement/video eyeball). Never mix both in one trend line or average.
- Inter-rater reliability: write a one-line behavioural anchor per rubric level; fixed protocol card (cone distances, feeder, attempts); same tester and surface per cycle; best-of-3 or mean-of-3; second coach re-scores 10% of athletes; log tester ID.
- Home-grown tests are unvalidated proxies. Badminton shuttle-run in published work is a "practice-based protocol" and peer reviewers noted it is not formally validated, and manual feeding adds error. Show as "internal benchmark", not norm. [3]
- Show an athlete's own change vs baseline first; peer percentile only after n >= ~15 in the same band+level, else hide.

## 2. Metrics per sport
Validation tiers: Coach = assessing coach; Second = second coach/head coach re-score; Auto = device/timing gate or app.

### Badminton
| Label | Unit | Test method | Higher better | Who validates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Serve accuracy | % of 10 serves in target zone | 10 short + 10 long serves to marked zones | Yes | Coach (Second on 10%) |
| Six-corner footwork | seconds (mean of 3) | Shuttle pick-up/shadow to 6 court corners via base, timed | No | Coach (stopwatch/Auto) |
| Rally consistency | count of clear/drop rallies without error | Cooperative rally with coach/partner, best of 3 | Yes | Coach |
| Smash/clear accuracy | % in target zone (10 fed) | Fed shuttles to rear court, target zone behind service line | Yes | Coach |
| Reaction split-step | rubric 1-5 (estimated) | Observe split-step timing in fed drill | Yes | Second |

### Football
| Label | Unit | Test method | Higher better | Who validates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Passing accuracy | % of 10 passes into 2 m gate at 10 m | Fixed distance, dominant/weaker foot separate | Yes | Coach |
| Dribble slalom | seconds | 9 cones, 2 m apart, out and back [4] | No | Coach/Auto |
| 20 m sprint | seconds (best of 2) | Flying or standing start, gates or phone timer | No | Auto preferred |
| Juggling | count of consecutive touches (best of 3) | Feet/thighs, no hand | Yes | Coach |
| Shooting accuracy | % on target of 10 | From 12-16 m (age-scaled) | Yes | Coach |
| Weaker-foot use | rubric 1-5 (estimated) | Observed in small-sided game | Yes | Second |

### Pickleball
| Label | Unit | Test method | Higher better | Who validates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Serve depth accuracy | % of 10 legal serves landing in back third | Target zone behind kitchen line | Yes | Coach |
| Third-shot drop | % of 10 landing in kitchen | Fed from baseline | Yes | Coach |
| Dink rally | count of consecutive dinks | Cooperative cross-court dinks, best of 3 | Yes | Coach |
| Return depth | % past service midline of 10 | Fed serves | Yes | Coach |
| Court movement | seconds (mean of 3) | Baseline-to-kitchen ladder shuttle | No | Coach/Auto |

### Cricket (box cricket)
| Label | Unit | Test method | Higher better | Who validates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bowling accuracy | % of 12 balls in target zone (good-length box) | Fixed run-up, marked zone | Yes | Coach |
| Batting contact | % of 12 fed balls hit in control | Underarm/machine feed, coach judges "in control" | Yes | Coach (estimated if judged) |
| Throwing accuracy | hits of 6 at stumps from 15 m | Fixed distance | Yes | Coach |
| Catching | catches of 10 | Fixed-height feeds | Yes | Coach |
| Run between wickets | seconds | Two-run shuttle | No | Coach/Auto |

No federation-published benchmark values (e.g. "good serve % at U12") found for any sport; set platform thresholds from your own academy data and mark as internal.

## 3. KPI table
| KPI | Formula | Include / Exclude | Pitfall | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Active athletes | Distinct athletes with >=1 attended session or paid active plan in last 30 days | Excl. trials, paused, alumni, duplicates across sports (count once; also show per-sport) | Multi-sport athletes double counted | None found |
| Retention (period) | (Active at end who were active at start) / Active at start | Excl. new joiners in period | Counting new joiners inflates it | HFA 2025 average annual retention reported as 66.4% for gyms (secondary source) [7]; gym-only, not youth sport |
| Retention (cohort) | % of an enrolment-month cohort still active at month 3/6/12 | Cohort by first paid enrolment month | Small cohorts noisy; seasonal intake (June school start) | None found |
| Churn | 1 - period retention; lost athletes / start actives | Count as lost: no attendance 30-60 days + no payment; exclude planned pauses and graduations | Definition of "lost" varies; pauses mislabelled | IHRSA: average club annual attrition 28.6% (gyms, older) [6] |
| Attendance rate | Sessions attended / sessions scheduled for enrolled athletes | Excl. cancelled sessions and excused/injury absences (report separately) | Denominator includes cancellations | None found |
| Enquiry to trial | Trials booked / unique enquiries (cohort by enquiry month) | Excl. duplicates, spam | Time lag: use cohort not same-month ratio | None found |
| Trial to enrolment | Paid enrolments / trials attended (within 30 days) | Excl. no-shows (track separately) | Using booked rather than attended trials | None found |
| Fee collection rate | Fees collected / fees due in period (INR) | Excl. waived/scholarship from due; include late payments in collected only when received | Cash vs UPI timing; waivers hidden | Club software cites "collection success rate" as a core KPI but gives no number [8] |
| Outstanding dues | Sum of overdue invoices; age buckets 0-7/8-30/31+ days | Excl. disputed (flag) | Netting advance payments | None found |
| DSO | (Outstanding receivables / billed in period) x days in period | Billing-based, not prepaid-only | Meaningless for fully prepaid plans; use only for post-paid | None found |
| Assessment completion | Athletes with assessment this cycle / athletes eligible (>=4 weeks enrolled) | Excl. injured/absent with reason | Completion without quality (all 3s) | None found |
| Revenue per athlete | Revenue in period / average active athletes | Excl. one-off merchandise and tournament pass-throughs (show separately); GST excluded | Mix of plan types and multi-sport athletes | None found |

PlayMetrics lists active participation and player retention among core youth-club KPIs [9]; no numeric youth benchmark given.
